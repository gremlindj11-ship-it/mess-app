# Building and reviewing Mess

Before designing, implementing or reviewing a feature in the app, website or an integration, read and apply [.agents/skills/feature-teardown/SKILL.md](.agents/skills/feature-teardown/SKILL.md). This includes work proposed in reviews, backlogs and existing PRs. Use its bounded repair path for small fixes.

The user's current scope, pauses and authorization take precedence. The skill does not authorize product changes while the user has asked to stop at research or planning. Content-only fact checks follow the applicable content instructions and the user's role boundaries.

Keep skill files and working notes out of deployed website assets. Do not claim a capability, live integration test, clinical review, merge or deployment happened without evidence.
