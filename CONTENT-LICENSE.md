## What it covers
Only Mess's own writing, made from open sources and published by Mess:
- The statements and stories written for Mess in `content/open/` (`statements.src.txt`, `statements.csv`, `story.json` and the like), and what is built from them for the app
- The notes in `app/assets/notes.js` and the stories in `app/assets/stories.js`, as far as they are Mess's own writing
- Packs made by Mess from that writing, and packs contributed to the Mess library unless they say otherwise

## What it never covers
- **Anything in `private/`.** That includes `private/frozen/`, which holds what was made from NICE, BTS, SIGN or GOLD guidance and was taken off the site on 26 Nov 2026.
- **The old guideline folders in `content/`** (every folder under `content/` other than `open/`, `open-sources/` and `gmc/`), `content/sources/`, and anything listed in `content/rights-frozen.json`. They rest on guidance whose owners' terms apply, and Mess does not licence them to anyone.
- **The open sources themselves**, including the exact quotes Mess keeps beside each statement and the saved pages in `content/open-sources/`. They keep their own licences, listed in `content/rights.json`.

## What it lets anyone do
- **Share:** copy and redistribute it in any format, including commercially.
- **Adapt:** translate, remix and build on it.

As long as they:
- **Credit Mess** (for example "From Mess, messmed.co.uk, CC BY-SA 4.0") and say if they changed it.
- **Share alike:** release their version under the same licence, so it stays open.

## Not covered either
- **The code.** That's AGPL v3, in [LICENSE](LICENSE).
- **The Mess name and logo.**
- **Curriculum labels from their publishers.** For example, condition and presentation names from the *MLA content map* are © General Medical Council and reproduced with acknowledgement, as the GMC permits.
- **Third-party libraries and linked resources.** They keep their own licences.

## Contributing
By contributing notes, stories, statements or packs to Mess, you agree to license your contribution under CC BY-SA 4.0. Contribute only your own words, with sources, and never anything copied from textbooks, question banks or other resources, or anything identifying a patient.

Medical content in Mess is for education. It is checked against sources, but it isn't a substitute for current guidance or clinical judgement.
