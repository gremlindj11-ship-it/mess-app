As long as they:

- **Share alike:** release their version under the same licence, so it stays open.

## Contributing
By contributing notes, stories, claims or packs to Mess, you agree to license your contribution under CC BY-SA 4.0. Contribute only your own words, with sources, and never anything copied from textbooks, question banks or other resources, or anything identifying a patient.

Medical content in Mess is for education. It is checked against sources, but it isn't a substitute for current guidance or clinical judgement.
