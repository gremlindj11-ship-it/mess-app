Bring your own AI

Mess doesn't sell you one. Plug in the AI you already use and it works from your notes: it teaches the story, plays the patient and explains the mistake.

  • With a keyYour AI answers inside Mess, on the page you're on.
  • On your own computerA local model works too. Nothing leaves your machine.
  • No keyMess hands your AI the question and files the answer when you bring it back.
  • No AI at allStories, cards, mistakes and stats all work without one.

Why it's free

Question banks, flashcard apps, note subscriptions: it adds up fast. Passing finals shouldn't depend on what you can afford, so I built Mess for the students who feel that most. I'm in my final year of medicine at Manchester.

– Sofia

Mess is a study tool. It isn't clinical guidance, so check anything you'd act on against current guidelines and the BNF.
